Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Emmanuel Costa (“we,” “us”) collects, uses, and protects information submitted through trainwithcosta.com.

Information we collect

When you submit a coaching application, we collect the information you choose to provide, which may include:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and age
  • The coaching service you are interested in
  • Your fitness goals, experience level, training availability, and preferred training location
  • Information about injuries or physical limitations you choose to share
  • Any other details you include in your application

How we use your information

We use this information only to review your application, contact you about coaching, and prepare an appropriate coaching plan. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

How your application is delivered

Applications are transmitted by Web3Forms, a form-delivery service, which forwards your submission to Emmanuel by email. Web3Forms processes this information under its own privacy policy.

Health-related information

Information about injuries, limitations, or health that you share is used only to help plan safe, appropriate training. Coaching does not replace diagnosis, treatment, prescriptions, or medical care from a licensed healthcare professional.

Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Links to Instagram take you to a third-party platform governed by its own policies.

Data retention

We keep application information only as long as needed to respond to you and provide coaching, or as required by law.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the information you submitted by emailing costaifbbpro@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to costaifbbpro@gmail.com.
